Pricing for launch

How the team landed on a simple launch price.

6 sources4 contributorsupdated Sep 12
The call
Launch with one simple paid plan at $49/mo (free for small teams) — not a multi-tier pack. Source changed
Why it mattered
Customers will pay for trusted knowledge, not another tool. A simple plan ships faster, stays explainable in one breath, and lets us learn before packaging gets fancy.
